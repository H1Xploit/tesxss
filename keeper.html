Format resmi dari wallet-api · TransactionRequest lengkap

⚙️ Config

1️⃣ txrequest-inline (format resmi)

Format: https://app.tonkeeper.com/v1/txrequest-inline/<base64url(TransactionRequest)>
TransactionRequest = {version, body:{type, expires_sec, response_options, params}}

-

2️⃣ txrequest-url (format resmi)

Format: https://app.tonkeeper.com/v1/txrequest-url/<domain/path.json>
URL yang di-fetch harus mengembalikan JSON TransactionRequest.

-

3️⃣ Signer Register

Format: tonkeeper://signer/link?pk=<hex>&name=<name>

-

4️⃣ Signer Sign (body harus HEX)

Dokumentasi signer-ios: body=<transaction_body_hex> (hex, bukan base64)

-

5️⃣ Publish (signer return)

Format: tonkeeper://publish?sign=<signature_hex>
